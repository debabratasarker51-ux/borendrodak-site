About the Foundation

Our mission is to support patients and families with trusted coordination—fast, respectful, and community-driven.

Who we are

BorendroDak Health & Welfare Foundation is a community-based initiative in Rajshahi that focuses on patient coordination and welfare support. We help connect people to services—doctors, blood support, emergency medicine, and transport—through a single hotline.

Mission

Reduce delays and confusion during health emergencies by coordinating help quickly and responsibly.

Values

Transparency, dignity, speed, privacy, and community partnership.
