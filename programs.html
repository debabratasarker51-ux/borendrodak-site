Programs & Services

Clear, practical programs—designed for speed, safety, and dignity during emergencies and everyday needs.

What we support

These are the most common service requests we coordinate through our hotline and community network.

🩺

Doctor appointment coordination

Helping patients book appointments, find relevant specialists, and navigate where to go.

Best for: guidance + scheduling
💊

Emergency medicine support

Support to locate uncommon or urgent medicines through pharmacy contacts and quick coordination.

Best for: urgent medicine search
🩸

Blood support

Connecting donors, blood banks, and patient attendants to reduce time loss during emergencies.

Best for: donor + blood bank linking
🚑

Emergency transport

Assisting with transport coordination so patients can reach facilities faster when time is critical.

Best for: transport coordination
🏥

Admission & operation guidance

Coordination support and guidance for hospital admission and operation-related steps.

Best for: hospital process help
📞

Telemedicine support

When possible, we help people access phone/video consultation or guidance before visiting a facility.

Best for: remote guidance
